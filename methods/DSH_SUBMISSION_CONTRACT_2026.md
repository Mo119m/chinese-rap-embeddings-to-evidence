# Digital Scholarship in the Humanities submission contract

Author guidelines checked 25 August 2026: https://academic.oup.com/dsh/pages/general_instructions

- Full paper normally no more than 9,000 words, excluding notes and references.
- English only; double-spaced manuscript text.
- Structured abstract no more than 250 words with: Purpose; Design/methodology/approach; Findings; Originality; Contribution to the field of Digital Humanities.
- Up to 12 short keywords.
- Main manuscript and figure files prepared separately; figures may remain embedded only for peer-review convenience.
- Oxford HUMSOC author–year style throughout: no comma between author and year in parenthetical citations; article and chapter titles in single quotation marks; journal volume/issue written as `volume/issue`; abbreviated page ranges.
- No author-inserted page or line numbers.
- Artwork at least 300 dpi; line/colour figures should target 600 dpi. TIFF is preferred for bitmap figures.
- Each figure requires a legend and an `Alt text:` description directly below the legend in the manuscript.
- Submission figures contain no duplicated `Figure X`, headline, caption, or long boundary paragraph inside the artwork. Figure legends and alt text are collected on a separate manuscript page; files are named `fig1.tif` through `fig4.tif`.
- Visible artwork text is at least 7 pt at intended print size; colour line/composite figures are supplied at 600 dpi, with vector PDF/SVG companions when available.
- A `Data availability` statement is required.
- An `AI Disclosure Statement` is required when AI/ML tools assisted manuscript preparation; it must name the tools/versions, describe their use, and confirm human checking and approval. The submission cover letter must also disclose this use.
- Add funding, conflict-of-interest, ethics, and author-contribution statements as appropriate; leave real author/affiliation/funding details for the human authors rather than inventing them.
- Use title case for numbered headings and explicitly pinned Times New Roman styles so Word theme fonts cannot substitute Calibri.
- Do not reproduce lyric passages in the manuscript or release unless permissions permit; aggregate and short analytic tokens only.
